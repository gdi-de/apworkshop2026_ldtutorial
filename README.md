# apworkshop2026_ldtutorial
Tutorial Linked Open Data AP Workshop 2026

This tutorial showcases the conversion of two Geodata sets retrieved from German geospatial agency websites into RDF (Linked Open Data) and how to deal with the results of such a conversion in practice.

## Use Case 1: Baumkataster Hamburg (cadastre of trees alongside a street)
Trees in Germany are numbered and classified according to the kind of tree, their position within an urban environment, and finally some parameters of the tree which help  to characterize its shape.

Use Case 1 examines how to convert these parameters into Linked Open Data.

## Use Case 2: Trinkwasserbrunnen Berlin (drinking water fountains)

Drinking water fountains are a common occurrence in many German cities, including Berlin.

Drinking water fountains are classified by the type of drinking water fountain, their inception, their accessibility, and their location.
